## Score Fields

- `First Open`: 1-5, where 5 means the tester knew what to do within 30 seconds.
- `Mira Comfort`: 1-5, where 5 means Mira felt helpful and not distracting.
- `Rest Clarity`: 1-5, where 5 means the tester understood and completed rest without help.
- `Keep 1 Week`: `yes`, `maybe`, or `no`.

## Categories

Use one primary category:

- `install-trust`
- `first-value`

## Weekly Review

Each weekly review should answer:

1. How many testers installed without help?
2. How many understood first value within 30 seconds?
3. How many completed one rest flow?
4. How many said Mira felt comfortable?
5. How many would keep EyeFlow installed for one week?
6. What are the top three repeated issues?
7. Which issue blocks widening the beta?

## Widening Gate

Move from trusted testers to public beta only when:

- Install success is at least 5/6.
- First-open clarity average is at least 4.2.
- Mira comfort average is at least 4.0.
- Rest clarity average is at least 4.0.
- At least half of testers say `yes` to keeping EyeFlow installed for one week.

If any gate fails, keep the cohort small and ship one focused fix before inviting more testers.
