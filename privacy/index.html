Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

EyeFlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything the app knows about your rhythm stays on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. EyeFlow has no account, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that profile you.

What stays on your device

Your settings and your eye-rest rhythm (such as reminder preferences and today's break count) are stored only in the app's local container on your Mac. They never leave your device, and we cannot see them.

Network use

The App Store version of EyeFlow runs entirely offline for its core function. It does not send your information anywhere.

Children

EyeFlow is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email eyeflow@buttondown.email.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 22 日

EyeFlow 不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据。App 关于你节奏的一切，只留在你的 Mac 上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。EyeFlow 没有账号、无需登录，也没有任何接收你数据的服务器。我们不使用分析、广告、追踪，也不接入会给你画像的第三方 SDK。

什么留在你的设备上

你的设置与用眼节奏（如提醒偏好、当天休息次数）只保存在本机 App 的本地容器里。它们从不离开你的设备，我们也看不到。

网络使用

App Store 版 EyeFlow 的核心功能完全离线运行，不会把你的信息发送到任何地方。

儿童

EyeFlow 适合所有年龄使用，且不向任何人（包括儿童）收集数据。

变更
